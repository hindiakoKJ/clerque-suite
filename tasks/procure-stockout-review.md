# Cafe Carolina: can a customer still be told no?

## 1. Can it still happen?

Yes. The most likely way at this shop: an ingredient with no reorder level — which is most of them — drifts to zero over a quiet weekend, and **nothing in the system is capable of telling anyone**, because the only job that runs on its own queries the finished-goods table (`apps/api/src/notifications/notifications.scheduler.ts:57` — `prisma.inventoryItem.findMany`, and `grep -rn rawMaterial apps/api/src/notifications/` returns zero hits) and her drinks are recipe-based with no rows there. Every ingredient warning in the product is *pull*: it exists only if a human opens Procure and taps. Monday at 10am the milk is gone, the latte tile greys out, and the barista says no — with the system having been correct the whole time and having told nobody.

## 2. What already protects her

These are real and she can lean on them.

- **The tile is the last line of defence and it works.** `maxProducible` is recomputed from live ingredient stock on every fetch (`apps/api/src/products/products.service.ts:707-747`), the terminal repolls every 15s (`apps/web/app/pos/(pos)/terminal/page.tsx:140`), the tile is hard-disabled at zero (`apps/web/components/pos/ProductGrid.tsx:268-273`), and it **names the limiting ingredient on the tile face** — "needs Fresh Milk" (`ProductGrid.tsx:365-370`). Selling an iced mocha on one tablet lowers the hot mocha's number on the other.
- **An empty stockroom is visible.** `getLowStock` reads `rawMaterial` and left-joins branch stock (`apps/api/src/inventory/inventory.service.ts:395-401`), so an ingredient never received counts as 0 rather than vanishing.
- **Nothing silently erases anything.** Sales (`orders.service.ts:1242-1246`), receipts (`inventory.service.ts:1690`) and prep (`sub-recipes.service.ts:551-585`) are all relative writes with a floor pass. Two tills, or a delivery landing mid-rush, cannot cancel each other out.
- **"Found nothing" is distinguishable from "nobody is watching."** Check stock counts ingredients with no reorder level (`procure.service.ts:208-212`) and says so on screen (`apps/web/app/procure/requests/page.tsx:158-166`). That is the right instinct and it works.
- **Buying twice cannot double stock** when a reference is given (`inventory.service.ts:1596-1625`), and **a bad line does not cost the whole delivery** — Procure posts line by line and refuses to close while any line failed (`procure.service.ts:302-346`).
- **Substitutions are honest.** An oat latte nets modifier lines against the base recipe before draining, so it consumes no dairy and is not billed for both (`orders.service.ts:1105-1140`).
- **Prep is real accounting when it is recorded**: FIFO lots drained oldest-first, both sides written to the ledger, cost blended into the prep's WAC (`sub-recipes.service.ts:542-548, 572-585, 660-690`).
- **A crazy delivery cost is refused before it rewrites every drink's margin** (`inventory.service.ts:1647-1661`), and a cycle count applies its variance *relative* to live stock so counting during trading does not reverse the day's sales (`warehouse.service.ts:436-455`).

## 3. Where it breaks — ranked by how likely it is to bite this shop

1. **No ingredient shortage ever pushes a notification.** The 3am job reads `inventoryItem`, not `rawMaterial` — `apps/api/src/notifications/notifications.scheduler.ts:57`. *Fix: add a producer that calls `inventory.getLowStock` per branch and raises the same broadcast.*
2. **Nothing caps a sale at what can be made.** Six lattes rung with three left: no server check, `Math.max(before - consumeQty, 0)` at `apps/api/src/orders/orders.service.ts:1165` floors the shortfall (it even computes `shortfallQty` at `:1213` and spends it on COGS), the tile only disables at exactly 0, and CartPanel's "+" has no cap (`apps/web/components/pos/CartPanel.tsx:168-174`). Three customers are refused holding a paid receipt. *Fix: throw naming the ingredient and the real ceiling unless `allowSaleWhenOutOfStock`; cap the tile and cart at `maxProducible`.*
3. **The prep chain cannot be switched on from the app at all.** `PUT /inventory/sub-recipes/:id` (`apps/api/src/sub-recipes/sub-recipes.controller.ts:64`) is the only writer of `SubRecipeItem`; nothing in `apps/web` calls it and the importer has no sheet for it — so for a prep-ahead shop the syrup's sugar never leaves the shelf, never trips a level, never reaches a buy list. *Fix: a recipe editor on the ingredient screen, or seed her syrups/sauces over the API at onboarding.*
4. **Prep items go on the grocery list as things to buy.** No prep exclusion in `getLowStock` (`inventory.service.ts:395-401`) or `pullLowStock` (`procure.service.ts:161-163`), so "White Sugar Syrup — SHORT 800 ml" prints on the slip; the fix (make a batch) is asked for by no screen. *Fix: tag `isPrep` and route to a Make action.*
5. **Modifier ingredients are deducted at sale but invisible to the ceiling.** `products.service.ts:688-693` collects raw materials from `bomItems` only and the modifier include at `:633-642` omits `ingredients`; the picker has zero stock awareness. Oat milk hits zero behind a green "22 left". *Fix: include option ingredients in the ceiling and grey out the option.*
6. **A repeated reference number makes a receive a silent no-op that the screen calls a success.** Server returns `duplicate: true, "Nothing was added."` (`inventory.service.ts:1610-1624`); the screen discards the response and fires `toast.success` unconditionally (`apps/web/app/pos/(pos)/inventory/page.tsx:290-311`). Type "Shopee" twice and the bottles are on the shelf, not in the app. *Fix: read `duplicate` and warn — Procure already does this at `procure.service.ts:322-326`.*
7. **"Sent to the owners" sends nothing.** `sendRequest` flips a status and stops (`procure.service.ts:230-234`); no module outside `procure/` references `purchaseRequest`. The barista stops worrying; the owner never learns. *Fix: call NotificationsService, or change the toast to "List closed — show it to the owner".*
8. **Check stock says "Nothing is below its reorder level" when items are low but already on a stale unsent list.** `procure.service.ts:171` skips them without counting, so `added` is 0; open requests never expire (`:54-59`, `cutoffAt` at `schema.prisma:5752` is read by nothing). *Fix: return `alreadyListed` and flag a request older than a shop-day.*
9. **An ingredient exactly at its reorder level is never bought, and a level of 0 hides it forever.** `if (!(shortBy > 0)) continue;` at `procure.service.ts:170`, while `getLowStock` flags on `<=` (`inventory.service.ts:421-422`); the home banner also filters `> 0` (`apps/web/app/procure/page.tsx:51`) and the unmonitored count only catches `null` (`procure.service.ts:208-210`). Cups, lids and filters land on round numbers routinely. *Fix: `if (shortBy < 0) continue;` and fold `lowStockAlert: 0` into the unmonitored count.*
10. **The buy quantity has no relationship to how fast she uses it or how long delivery takes.** `qtyRequested = shortBy * 2` (`procure.service.ts:185`), and there is no field to do better with — `RawMaterial` has no lead time (`packages/db/prisma/schema.prisma:1332-1376`) and `PurchaseRequest` no expected-arrival (`:5742-5771`), though `sentAt`/`receivedAt` already hold every past Shopee lead time and nothing reads them. Beans at 500 g/day, level 300 g, stock 250: the list asks for **100 g** against a 3-day wait. *Fix: `avgDailyUse × (leadTime + cover) − onHand`, floored at the current rule.*
11. **The request closes RECEIVED when lines were merely skipped, and there is no way back in.** `allDone = failed.length === 0` at `procure.service.ts:340`; the skipped lines are destructured and never shown (`requests/page.tsx:229-235`), the banner says "All in stock", and `recordBought` then rejects her (`:247-251`). The evening Shopee parcel lands nowhere. *Fix: treat "Nothing was bought for this line" like a failure and keep the request open.*
12. **A real price move dead-ends the correction.** No bulk path passes `acceptCostChange` (`procure.service.ts:314-320`, `purchase-orders.service.ts:457-468`, `close-and-plan.service.ts:357-365`) and the "the price really changed" tick exists only on the Ingredients screen; worse, after a total failure the request stays BOUGHT but the Save button renders only for SENT (`requests/page.tsx:624-626`), so retyping the pack size goes nowhere. A 25 kg sack of rice stays out of stock with the sack in the kitchen. *Fix: accept the flag on both bulk receive endpoints.*
13. **PO receive commits the paperwork before it posts the stock, and the failure is invisible.** Quantities and status are written inside the transaction (`purchase-orders.service.ts:373-425`), stock is posted after it at `:455-479`, and the screen never reads `failed` (`apps/web/app/admin/(admin)/purchase-orders/[id]/page.tsx:71-82`); re-receiving is blocked by `:350-352`. PO says RECEIVED, shelf unchanged, no route back. *Fix: only advance a line whose `receiveRawMaterial` actually returned.*
14. **Offline sales never move the cached ceiling.** `saveOffline` writes to Dexie and returns with no decrement (`terminal/page.tsx:360-381`); polling stops (`:140`) and the grid falls back to the last stamped payload (`:134`). 30 drinks later the tile still shows the 8:59am number. *Fix: decrement cached `maxProducible` in Dexie, or subtract queued orders before render.*
15. **The cook cannot open the prep board and the baristas cannot find it.** `GENERAL_EMPLOYEE` is absent from `@Roles` on `list()` (`sub-recipes.controller.ts:25-26`, verified) so the page 403s; the only link is gated to stock roles (`apps/web/app/procure/page.tsx:109`) and Procure has no sidebar. Unrecorded prep = components that never move. *Fix: add `GENERAL_EMPLOYEE` to the roles and show the tile to everyone the route already admits.*
16. **Nobody on shift can 86 an item.** `ProductGrid.tsx:273` is the only gate; there is no per-product availability flag in the schema, and a CASHIER is barred from every adjustment endpoint (`inventory.controller.ts:211, 289`). Drop the vanilla bottle and every vanilla drink stays green until the owner logs in. *Fix: an `isAvailable` flag toggled by long-press, auto-reset at shift close.*
17. **The till's amber warning is "5 cups left", not her reorder point.** `lowStockAlert` is read from an `InventoryItem` row a recipe product does not have (`products.service.ts:754`), so it falls back to `maxProducible <= 5` (`:758`) — minutes of runway — and `limitedBy` is never consulted for the threshold. *Fix: derive `isLowStock` from the limiting ingredient's own level.*
18. **The bulk stock-receipt importer writes stock absolutely** — `update: { quantity: new Prisma.Decimal(newOnHand) }` at `apps/api/src/import/import.service.ts:3327`, the only remaining absolute write. Import during trading and the sold units come back, so the tile reads high and the barista accepts an order she cannot make. *Fix: `{ increment: qty }`.*
19. **If she runs the tablet app, there is no last line of defence at all.** `toFbProduct` drops `isOutOfStock` and `limitedBy` (`apps/counter/src/terminal/fb/FBTerminal.tsx:28-35`) and `handleProductPress` has no guard, so an unmakeable drink reads "Low · 0" and sells — while the phone shell of the same app does it correctly (`PhoneSellScreen.tsx:186-190`). *Fix: carry both fields through and disable.*
20. **The prep board names the limiter with no way to act on it** — "Out of Brown Sugar" is a static `<p>` (`apps/web/app/procure/batches/page.tsx:320-333`) — and the Procure home tile prints an unqualified "Nothing is below its reorder level" with none of the unmonitored caveat Check stock carries (`apps/web/app/procure/page.tsx:169`). *Fix: an "Add to buy list" button, and the caveat on the home tile.*

## 4. The one change

**Make the 3am job read ingredients, and make it say three numbers: what is at or below its level, what is at zero, and how many ingredients nobody is watching.** Everything else on this list is downstream of nobody looking. The reorder levels are static, the buy quantity is a placeholder, the syrup is on the wrong list — all true, and all of it only matters *after* a human opens the app. Today nothing ever makes one. The counter's amber badge is the sole warning any person receives without deciding to go looking, and it fires at five cups: at 500 g of beans a day that is roughly fifteen minutes, against a three-day Shopee wait. That is not a warning, it is a countdown.

Every part needed already exists. `getLowStock` (`inventory.service.ts:395-433`) returns name, quantity, unit and shortBy in exactly the shape an alert body needs; `procure.service.ts:208-212` already counts the unmonitored ingredients; `notifications.scheduler.ts:79` already knows how to broadcast to a tenant with a link. It is one producer beside `lowStockProducer`, calling code that is already written and already tested. And the zero-stock and unmonitored counts do not depend on her having set a single reorder level, which is the one precondition she cannot meet today — so unlike every other fix here, this one starts working on the night it ships. Fix #2 next: it is the difference between a customer hearing "we're out" before they order and hearing it after they have paid.
