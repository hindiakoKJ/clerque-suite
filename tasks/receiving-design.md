# Receiving goods: one door for palengke, Shopee/Lazada and supplier deliveries

Design assessment, 2026-09-06. STATUS 2026-09-09: steps 1-6 BUILT and live (see tasks/todo.md); steps 7-9 open. Produced from four code readers, four independent designs, three judges, one synthesis, and a 49-claim fact check against the code (45 hold; the 4 refuted were precision fixes, noted at the end). No database column is required by any step.

## The recommendation

Build Design 4 (the purchase request is the single receiving record; post what ARRIVED at the price PAID per pack; money moves only at receive; no new column anywhere) but cut into Design 3's waves so every step ships alone and stays inside apps/api/src/procure until credit is confirmed by the owner. Grafts taken: Design 3's controller pass-through (ProcureService.receiveRequest already accepts opts.receivedAt/note and the controller drops them), lineIds ticks plus post-twice for a mixed-pocket trip, vendorBillRef as a one-line addition to the inventory receive DTO, postExpenses lifted out of the receipts service, and Other charges rows on the request card; Design 1's Procure-scoped POST /procure/requests/:id/photo (the Documents upload route excludes GENERAL_EMPLOYEE and WAREHOUSE_STAFF), parse-by-documentId, source badges on every prefilled number, and still-coming as a follow-up request created in SENT (never on the OPEN shopping list, which Check stock would re-buy); Design 2's APBillLines on the per-DR bill, billId in the event payload, voucher netting order (shipping first, then pro-rata into goods), 5030 Freight In behind one map entry, PATCH /ap/bills/:id/vendor-ref, CARD/BANK_TRANSFER payment labels, request-lines-first matching, and an explicit record-only confirm. Flaws fixed: attach-to-request writes only packsBought/packSize/packCost/brandNote onto kitchen lines and uses the max-suffix line-number rule (the replay branch today overwrites qtyRequested with packs x size and numbers new lines by count); partial arrival in v1 is whole-line ticks + packsArrived only where short + a SENT follow-up request, not lot-derived -P2 references (those rewrote line.receivedAt semantics and recordBought's already-posted guard); the receive body becomes a class-validator DTO so the global whitelist pipe validates it; CREDIT is refused before the loop when vendorId or the advancedAccounting plan feature is missing; a floor role cannot overwrite packs a decider typed; packCost 0 is refused on the hand path (today a zero blends into WAC); BANK is explicitly refused by the spreadsheet importer; side postings (fees, freight, Lost) are guarded by @RequireIdempotency on /receive plus posted-only-when-a-line-posted; the freight account is one map entry (5030 default, 6100 alternative) for the accountant to pick; short-delivery treatment (post what arrived, follow-up or Refunded/Lost) is presented to Anne as a decision, because the post-what-you-paid-for rule the brief cites is not written anywhere in tasks/ or memory. Total effort M in 9 steps; the first two steps are S, need no AI, no owner answer, and remove the wrong-date books, the vanishing unbought line, the retyped pack size and the BOUGHT-edits-discarded defect.

## One entry point

The branch's purchase request card at /procure/requests, opened on a phone browser, is the one door for all three channels. Same four moves every time: (1) build the list (any request role, tap ingredient, type quantity, or Check stock); (2) Send (owner/manager/MDM); (3) Record what came, by whoever is holding the bag, parcel or delivery receipt: Add photo (receipt / order screenshot / DR / SI, labelled), tick Arrived per line, adjust packs, correct a price, Save (works at SENT and BOUGHT); (4) Add what arrived to stock (owner/manager/MDM): pocket tile (From the till / Owner paid / Shop bank-GCash / On credit), date (prefilled from the receipt read, else the day packs were saved, else today), one note line, line ticks, Post. The receipts screen (/procure/receipts) stops being a separate door: it is reached only from the card as Read the photo, deciders only, and always writes onto the request it was opened from. Stock on hand stays for corrections and opening stock; the spreadsheet stays for bulk; the Counter app is untouched (web only).

## (a) Direct buying - palengke / grocery, cash, several stalls, thermal or handwritten or no receipt

Flow:
- 1. Build and Send exactly as today (cook taps ingredients / Check stock; owner taps Send). Nothing posts.
- 2. Back at the shop, whoever shopped opens the SENT request. Each line shows packs prefilled as ceil(qtyRequested / last pack size), One pack holds and last price prefilled from the newest received PurchaseRequestLine of that ingredient (lastPack query, badge 'last time: 1000 g at P68'), a same-family unit chip (kg/g, L/ml) on One pack holds, brand optional. They tick Arrived, fix packs and price where the paper differs, tap Add photo once per stall (Procure-scoped route, many photos per request, label chip Receipt), tap Save what was bought. Lines not bought stay blank. Something bought that was not on the list: Add what else you bought (ingredient tile + packs) creates a line with the next control number. Request -> BOUGHT; Save also works at BOUGHT (fixes the discarded-edits defect).
- 3. Owner/manager/MDM opens the BOUGHT request that night or next morning. Optional: Read the photo (Gemini Flash on Vertex, one read per photo against the 50/day cap) opens the receipts screen preloaded with the request lines; the deterministic matcher scores the request's own ingredients first and fills pack size / price / receipt date / OR no. onto the matching rows without touching the recorder's packs; a second stall's photo appends rows; unmatched printed lines are offered as extras or expenses (parking, delivery fee). Save writes onto the same request (purchaseRequestId + postNow:false) - no second request is created.
- 4. Post: pick the pocket (From the till = 1010, Owner paid = 3010, Shop bank/GCash = 1020 new), confirm the date (prefilled from the receipt read, else the day packs were saved, else today, source badged; backdatable to the market day), type one note line if wanted ('Aling Nena, no receipt'), leave all filled lines ticked, tap Add what arrived to stock. Mixed pockets: tick the till-paid lines, Post, then tick the rest and Post with the other pocket (lineIds).
- 5. Server: receiveRequest posts each ticked line through receiveRawMaterial with quantity = packs x size, unitCost = price / size, receivedAt = the chosen date, referenceNumber = line control number (idempotent), note = 'stall - OR no. - brand'; lines with nothing bought are carried to the branch's OPEN request (same qtyRequested/shortBy, P2002 caught and re-read) and returned as carried[]; the request closes RECEIVED only when nothing is unposted-and-not-carried; the toast names posted / carried / failed lines and the SENT footnote finally tells the truth.
- 6. A failed line (x10 guard, locked period) leaves the request BOUGHT with editable boxes and a working Save, so the fix-and-repost loop exists on this screen.

Staff type: OPEN: the quantity needed per line (unchanged). Whoever shopped: usually only a price that moved and a packs count that differs from the prefilled ceil(need / last size); One pack holds only for an ingredient never bought before (once); a unit chip tap for a kilo of a gram-counted item; brand optional. Owner at post: nothing typed on a same-day cash run; otherwise the date (picker) and one note line; the pocket tile, line ticks and Post are taps. With a printed receipt read: corrections where the row is amber (ingredient pick, a pack size where units cross families, a price read as 0). Never: base-unit quantities, unit cost, references, VAT, WAC, journal entries, the next list's carried lines.

Clerque derives: Pack size, last price and brand from the last received line of that ingredient (query over PurchaseRequestLine; no column); default packs from the requested quantity; kg->g / L->ml via unitFactor. quantity = packs x size, unitCost = price / size; VAT netting from tenant.taxStatus (inert for NON-VAT Carolina); WAC blend, recipe re-cost, lot with reference = line number (no double receive), x10 cost guard with the existing override. Posting date = the chosen date, not server now; period-lock checked against it. Stall / receipt no. into PurchaseRequest.notes and every lot note; photos filed as Document(entityType 'PurchaseRequest') per stall, hidden from staff when the cost switch is off (existing). Carry-forward of unbought lines to the OPEN request; the 'Asked for / bought / short' comparison from stored figures.

Books: Per posted line, dated the chosen market date, reference = line control number: Dr 1051 Raw Materials (INGREDIENT) or 6210 / 6070 (supplies, expensed) at net, Dr 1040 Input VAT only for a VAT tenant on a non-owner pocket, Cr 1010 Cash on Hand (till) / 3010 Owner's Capital (owner) / 1020 Cash in Bank (shop bank or GCash, new BANK branch in the journal credit ladder) at gross. Expense rows (parking, delivery fee) post as Simple Entries from the same pocket on the same date (TRANSPORT 6100 / OTHER 6140; freight-type rows to the freight map entry, 5030 default); an owner-funded expense keeps today's OWNER_CONTRIBUTION pair. Nothing posts at SENT or BOUGHT. The Ledger links each JE back to /procure/requests?view=REQ-... by the line-number prefix as today.

## (b) Shopee / Lazada - ordered online, paid up front (GCash/card) or COD, order page is the receipt, parcels days later and possibly split, shipping and platform fees, vouchers

Flow:
- 1. List built and Sent as today; the owner orders on the app.
- 2. Order day (optional, 20 s): owner opens the SENT request, Add photo -> screenshot of the order page, label Order; Read the photo (once the order-page prompt kind is live) fills seller, Order ID, order date, per line packs / size (from the variation text, existing pack-size rule) / unit price, shipping fee and platform fee as expense rows, voucher netted shipping-first then pro-rata into goods so pesos posted = pesos paid; footing checked against the amount paid. Save with postNow:false: lines written, boughtAt = order date, notes tagged [ORDER:Shopee - seller - id], nothing posted. The card now reads 'Ordered <date> - on the way' and the requests page prefers today's OPEN/SENT over an [ORDER:]-tagged BOUGHT request, so the parcel no longer occupies the screen. With AI off: packs (prefilled) and price per line, fee amounts, order no. in the note - by hand on the same card.
- 3. Parcel arrives: whoever unpacks opens the request, ticks the lines that are in the box, sets Packs arrived only on a line that came short (prefilled with packsBought), taps Save. Owner picks the pocket: Shop bank/GCash (prepaid from the business wallet -> 1020), Owner paid (personal GCash/card -> 3010), From the till (COD from the drawer -> 1010); date defaults to today (arrival); taps Add what arrived to stock.
- 4. Server posts the ticked lines at packsArrived x size for the line's pack price on the arrival date; the line's packsBought stays as the paid-for figure. For every line short by N packs the server creates ONE follow-up request in SENT (REQ-YYYYMMDD-NNN, notes 'Balance of REQ-...: still coming', one line per remainder with packs = N and the same size/cost prefilled) and names it in the result; the original request closes RECEIVED when its ticked lines posted. Shipping / platform fee rows post once, in the first call that posted a line, from the chosen pocket (postExpenses, guarded by @RequireIdempotency on /receive).
- 5. Second parcel: the follow-up request is the live SENT request; tick Arrived, Save, owner posts with the pocket - two taps, no read needed. Never coming: on the follow-up line tap Close short -> Refunded (nothing more posts: the pocket was credited only for what arrived) or Lost (Dr 6140 / Cr pocket for missing packs x pack price; owner-funded uses the OWNER_CONTRIBUTION pair). The dialog says exactly what the books will and will not show; a Dr 1036 Other Receivables claim is offered only if Anne says she chases refunds (owner question), off by default.
- 6. Money timing: nothing at order time and the pocket credited on the arrival day is the standing decision (purchase-orders.service.ts:434-446, 'an order is a commitment'); the order/payment date lives in boughtAt and the note for the bank reconciliation. An optional 1063 Advance Deposits step exists only if Anne cares about month-end cut-off (owner question), not built in v1.

Staff type: Order day: nothing when the screenshot reads cleanly; otherwise packs / price per line (size prefilled), the two fee amounts, the order no. in the note. Arrival: line ticks (taps), Packs arrived only for a short line, one pocket tap, Post. Second parcel: two taps. Close short: one tap, Refunded or Lost. Never: a vendor record (seller is text in notes), a fee account, VAT, the follow-up request, dates (order date read or defaulted; arrival = today).

Clerque derives: Order date -> boughtAt; seller and Order ID -> notes tag; 'on the way' label and default-view rule from that tag. Pack size from the variation text; unit price net of voucher (shipping first, then pro-rata, residue to the largest line; shown as 'Voucher P120 spread across the goods'); fees as expense rows. Per-line lots / WAC / JE dated each parcel's arrival; the remainder as a prefilled follow-up SENT request; Lost expense at pack price; the fee rows posted once. Read-only hint 'last Shopee order took N days' from sentAt -> receivedAt of past [ORDER:]-tagged requests (no column).

Books: Nothing on order day (locked decision). On each arrival, per ticked line, dated that day, reference = line control number: Dr 1051 (or 6210/6070) net (+ Dr 1040 for a VAT tenant on a non-owner pocket) / Cr 1020 Cash in Bank (Shop bank or GCash, new BANK value in RawMaterialLot.paymentMethod String? and the journal credit ladder), 1010 (COD from the till) or 3010 (owner's own wallet) at gross. Shipping and platform fee as Simple Entries with the first parcel: Dr 5030 Freight In (default map entry; 6100 alternative) / Cr the same pocket, gross, no VAT split (existing v1 rule). A line that never comes: Refunded -> nothing further (net cash out already equals net cash paid); Lost -> Dr 6140 / Cr pocket. Landed cost excludes shipping (expensed, not in WAC) unless Anne chooses otherwise.

## (c) Supplier delivery (Manila) - ordered by phone/Viber, arrives with a DR and/or sales invoice, on credit terms, sometimes partial, freight sometimes separate

Flow:
- 1. List built and Sent; owner taps Copy as message (the SENT lines as plain text with last pack words) and pastes it into Viber. The request IS the order; nothing posts. If the supplier quotes prices, the owner (or the cook, once /bought is open to floor roles) types the price per sack on the SENT lines; packs and size are prefilled from last time. Status BOUGHT = ordered, on the way; boughtAt = order date.
- 2. DR arrives (usually no prices). Whoever signs opens the request, Add photo -> DR, label Delivery receipt; ticks Arrived per line on this DR, sets Packs arrived only where the DR count differs, Save. Optional later: Read the photo with the delivery-receipt prompt kind fills DR no., date and delivered quantities (prices left null, never invented).
- 3. Owner/manager/MDM posts: pocket tile On credit - supplier bill (shown only when user.planFeatures.advancedAccounting is true; the server refuses CREDIT without the feature or without a vendor BEFORE any line posts) -> Supplier picker fed by GET /procure/vendors (+ New supplier by name via POST /procure/vendors, both behind PlanFeatureGuard; TIN/address later under Ledger), Terms days (default 30), DR/SI no. (goes to vendorBillRef; the note carries it too), freight row: amount + 'On the supplier's bill' ticked (Cr 2010) or unticked (paid to the trucker now, cash/bank Simple Entry), Add what arrived to stock.
- 4. Step 7 server behaviour: every ticked line goes through receiveRawMaterial with paymentMethod CREDIT, vendorId, termsDays, referenceNumber = line number, vendorBillRef = the DR/SI no. (new DTO field; inventory.service.ts:1932 becomes dto.vendorBillRef ?? dto.referenceNumber, so the (reference, gross) JE link keeps working); v1 result is one OPEN APBill per line, all tagged with the DR no., due = DR date + terms - the result screen says '8 bills created for DR 4471, pay them together under Ledger -> AP'. Freight on the bill posts through ApBillsService.create + post (BUSINESS_OWNER only) as a bill line on the freight map account; freight paid cash is an Other charges row.
- 5. Step 8 (one bill per DR): receiveRawMaterial gains an internal-only deferBill parameter (never on the HTTP DTO) and returns grossValue/inputVat; ProcureService writes ONE APBill per delivery numbered by NumberingService 'AP_BILL' (BILL-YYYY-####, ending the BILL-000NNN interleaving), reference = DR no., vendorBillRef = SI no. or null, dueDate = DR date + terms, createdById 'system-receive', with real APBillLines (account by material category, quantity, net unit price, taxAmount = inputVat, lineTotal = gross; freight as a 5030/6100 line), whtAmount 0 (owner question), and the billId carried in each line's RAW_MATERIAL_RECEIPT payload so the journal link can attach the line JEs; the AP bill page shows 'from Procure - see REQ-... for lines'.
- 6. Partial delivery: untouched lines stay unposted, the request stays BOUGHT with 'x of y lines arrived'; a line delivered short becomes a follow-up SENT request as in (b) with the supplier remembered on that follow-up's line notes; the second DR is received the same way and gets its own bill (one payable document = one bill). Lines the supplier will never send: Close short -> Not coming (no bill was raised for them; nothing to post on credit) and the request closes.
- 7. SI arrives later with its own number: Invoice number box on the request card -> PATCH /ap/bills/:id/vendor-ref (BUSINESS_OWNER, ACCOUNTANT, AP_ACCOUNTANT; allowed on system-receive bills; audit log), photo filed as Sales invoice against the same request. If the SI price differs from what was posted, v1 badges the request and bill 'price unconfirmed' and the accountant posts the delta by hand (owner question 4 decides whether a Correct-to-invoice step is worth building).
- 8. Pay under Ledger -> AP -> Payments as today; ap-payment DTO gains CARD and a BANK_TRANSFER label mapped to cash in bank (the service map already routes CARD there), so a Manila bank transfer stops being typed as QR_PH.

Staff type: Order: nothing beyond today's list (Copy as message is a tap); the price per sack only when the supplier quotes one and the last price moved. DR day: line ticks; Packs arrived only where the DR differs; supplier pick (once per supplier; a name for a new one); terms only if not 30; DR number; freight amount and one tick for where it is charged; the On credit tile; Post. SI day: the invoice number (one box) and one photo tap. Never: base-unit quantities, per-unit costs, bill numbers, due dates, the JE, the bill/JE link, the follow-up request.

Clerque derives: Viber order text from the request lines and last pack words. quantity and unit cost per line; VAT netting from tenant status; lot; JE Cr 2010; posting date = DR date. AP bill(s): vendor, due date from terms, vendorBillRef = DR/SI no., bill lines by material category (step 8), bill number from NumberingService, link from request to bill by reference. Partial-delivery state (request stays BOUGHT), follow-up SENT request for a short line, close-short. Freight JE on the bill or a cash Simple Entry from the freight map entry.

Books: Per posted line, dated the DR date, reference = line control number: Dr 1051 (or 6210/6070) net, Dr 1040 only for a VAT tenant (inert for NON-VAT Carolina: the whole invoice is cost), Cr 2010 Accounts Payable gross. AP sub-ledger: step 7 one OPEN bill per line carrying vendorBillRef = DR/SI no.; step 8 one bill per DR with APBillLines whose totals equal the sum of the line JEs' gross, VAT and net. Freight on the supplier's bill: Dr 5030 Freight In (default) or 6100 / Cr 2010 through the AP module's own create + post (source 'AP', because 2010 is AP_ONLY); freight paid cash at the door: Dr 5030/6100 / Cr 1010 or 1020 as a Simple Entry. WHT stays 0 on receive-created bills (a supplier expecting a 2307 uses the manual bill path). Payment later: Dr 2010 / Cr 1010 or cash in bank via AP Payments. The BIR Purchase Book still prints 'Stock: <ingredient>' and a blank TIN for lots until a follow-up joins the bill by reference (flagged, not in this cut).
